1. Collection and Use of Information
1.1 System Information Collection
We may collect system and application information from your terminal device in order to promptly notify you of available system and application updates. The information collected for this purpose includes but is not limited to device name, system and application version, operating system and browser version, region and language settings, device identification information (IMEI, ESN, MEID, SN), carrier network codes, etc. Our processing of the above information is limited to notifying you of available application updates.

1.2 Use of Personal Information
We will strictly comply with the content set forth in this Privacy Policy and its updates to use your personal information. Your personal information will only be used for the purposes determined at the time of collection and with your consent. If there is any other use beyond this, We will obtain your consent in advance. The information We collect will be used for the following purposes:
The personal information We collect will be used to register services for you or your device, and to provide the services or features you request;
To achieve the service purpose of "VCAM", the personal information collected by "VCAM" through technical means is limited to your phone's Android ID, phone's MAC address, basic information of the dashcam (We use the phone's Android ID, MAC address and the dashcam's ID to distinguish and analyze problems when the application encounters abnormalities and provide feedback), and usage time — all of which are necessary information.
Explanation of MAC sensitive information: We obtain the phone's MAC address to uniquely distinguish phone information, to collect APP problem logs and better analyze and fix issues for specific phone environments, thereby providing users with a better experience.
Explanation of IP address collection: When connecting to children's cameras and dashcams, the dashcam side requires the APP to register the phone's IP address with the dashcam to prevent other network devices that do not support the dashcam protocol from connecting. When the APP connects to the dashcam, it obtains the phone's current IP, which is a local area network IP assigned to the phone by the dashcam and does not affect user privacy.
Obtaining the phone's installed app list: We obtain the list of installed applications to enable users, when they choose to share videos and photos, to see the applications on the phone that provide sharing functionality, thereby achieving a better sharing experience.
Reading or deleting phone storage files: We need to obtain phone storage permission to save pictures and videos obtained from devices such as dashcams to a specific storage path on the phone, making them visible in the phone's gallery; and to provide users with the ability to delete these saved pictures and videos within the APP.
Obtaining running process information: We obtain information about currently running processes to determine whether the current APP is running in the foreground, so that the APP can handle device-related tasks when switching to the background (such as disconnecting live view, etc.).
Obtaining BSSID and SSID is used when the APP connects to Wi-Fi devices such as dashcams, to determine whether the Wi-Fi currently connected to the phone is the Wi-Fi of the dashcam or other device. If so, there is no need to jump to the system settings interface to connect to Wi-Fi, and communication can proceed directly through that Wi-Fi and the device.
Your mobile subscriber identification information, mobile device unique identifier, and geographic location/APP collected software list/device MAC address information may be used to activate specific features of the product or service you use. Such information is used to improve Our products and services, but We will not use such information to track your location;
We will strictly comply with relevant laws and regulations and the content set forth in this Privacy Policy to collect and use your information. Unless holding a written authorization document issued by Us, Our staff will not directly request personal information from you. If you encounter such a situation, you may contact Us directly.
